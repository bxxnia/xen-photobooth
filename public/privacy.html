XEN PHOTOBOOTH / PRIVACY

Your moments stay
yours.

We designed Xen Photobooth to be simple, private, and clear about what happens to your photos.

01

Camera access

The camera is used only when you start the photobooth and allow permission in your browser. You can deny access and upload images instead.

02

Your photos

Photos are processed in the browser while you use the booth. The app does not require an account and does not intentionally store your captured images on a permanent server.

03

Downloads and saved files

When you download or save a photo, the file is sent to your device through your browser. You control where that file is kept and when it is deleted.

04

QR codes

The QR code points to the photobooth page or share address used by the app. It does not contain your photo itself or personal account information.

05

Third-party services

The app may load fonts or other resources from external services. Those services may receive standard browser request information when the resources are loaded.
