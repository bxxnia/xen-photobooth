Xen Photobooth is a simple online photobooth made for capturing fun, cute, and memorable moments. Take your photos, create your own photo strip, and keep the memories with you.

01

What is Xen Photobooth?

Xen Photobooth is a web-based photobooth where you can take photos using your device camera without needing to create an account.

It is designed to be easy, fun, and accessible. Whether you're taking photos alone, with friends, or with someone special, Xen Photobooth gives you a simple way to turn those moments into something you can keep.
